01 · Before I come round

A quick chat
about the job

Let me know what needs doing, roughly how big it is, and what sort of wall or timber I'll be working with. That's usually plenty for me to tell you how long it'll take.

If it turns out to be a two-minute tweak you can do yourself with a screwdriver, I'll happily talk you through it on the phone.

02 · On the day

Dust sheets down,
tools out

I bring my own tools and a full kit of wall plugs, screws and brackets. Floors and nearby furniture get covered before any drilling or planing starts.

If I uncover a problem behind the plaster or under a board once I start, I'll stop and show you first so you can decide how you want to handle it.

03 · Before I leave

Checked over and cleaned up

I make sure everything sits level and opens smoothly, and I sweep up any dust and leave the area tidy. If anything settles or needs a tweak in the weeks after, give me a ring and I'll pop back.
